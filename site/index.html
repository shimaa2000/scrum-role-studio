Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers are Scrum accountabilities. A Project Manager may support delivery alongside them, with authority defined by the organization.

Comparison of Project Manager, Product Owner, and Scrum Master responsibilities
LensProject Manager Organizational roleProduct Owner Scrum accountabilityScrum Master Scrum accountability
Primary focusCoordinating project delivery within agreed constraints.Maximizing product value.Establishing Scrum and enabling team effectiveness.
Typical decisionsPlans, escalation, and governance within delegated authority.Product Goal and Product Backlog ordering.Coaching approaches and help with effective Scrum practices.
Useful evidenceMilestones, budget, risk and dependency visibility.Customer outcomes, value, and clear backlog direction.Improved collaboration, self-management, and resolved impediments.

Boundary to rememberDoes not take over Scrum accountabilities by job title.Does not dictate implementation or sprint capacity.Does not assign tasks or order the Product Backlog.

Keep Developers in the picture. They create and adapt the Sprint Backlog, select achievable work with the PO, and determine how to build it. The entire Scrum Team is accountable for a useful, valuable Increment.
